Company Screening Rules:

1. Prefer companies with stable product/business model and clear engineering ownership.
2. Be cautious if there are strong recent signals of layoffs, severe hiring freezes, or major financial distress.
3. Consider role quality at the company:
   - backend/system ownership opportunity
   - mentorship and engineering standards
   - realistic scope and growth potential
4. Avoid over-penalizing missing data; if evidence is mixed or limited, mark as Neutral.
5. If company risk is high, recommendation should lean to Consider/Skip even when role skills match.
